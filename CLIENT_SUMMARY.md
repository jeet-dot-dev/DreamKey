# Project Progress Summary

This is a simplified overview of the modules and features implemented in your application.

---

## 🏠 1. Properties (Stock) Module
Allows you to manage all property listings in one place.
*   **Add Listings:** A clean form to add new properties with automated checks to prevent typing errors.
*   **Property Details:** Detailed view pages to inspect properties.
*   **Property Management:** View list grids, edit information, and delete properties when sold or no longer needed.

## 🤝 2. Owner Module
Helps manage property owners and your relationship with them.
*   **Owner Lists:** A list view to see all owners and go into their detail pages.
*   **Favorites & Archives:** Mark important owners as favorites or archive inactive ones.
*   **Owner Stats:** Interactive dashboard widgets showing key numbers about owners.

## 💼 3. Broker Module
Helps keep track of partner agents and brokers.
*   **Broker Dashboard:** A central view for managing brokers and seeing key metrics.
*   **Broker Archives & Favorites:** Save frequent broker partners to favorites or archive old ones.

## 📞 4. Interaction History Module
Tracks all communication to maintain good relationships.
*   **Interaction Logs:** Log conversations, notes, and dates for both owners and brokers.
*   **Log Management:** Add details of phone calls or meetings, and delete outdated logs.

## 🎯 5. Leads Module
A dedicated space to track potential clients.
*   **Leads Dashboard:** Dedicated layout and overview screen to monitor incoming leads and convert them into deals.

## 🛑 6. System-Wide Delete Security
*   **Confirmation Modal:** A safety pop-up window that appears whenever you try to delete a property, owner, broker, or log. This prevents accidental deletion of important data.
