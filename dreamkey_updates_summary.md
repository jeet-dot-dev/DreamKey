# DreamKey CRM: Module Updates Summary

Here is a simple summary of the new features added to your DreamKey CRM system.

---

## 1. Customer Interaction History (Timeline Logs)
Keep track of all communications with your clients in one organized place.

* **Log Activities:** Easily record phone calls, WhatsApp messages, emails, meetings, site visits, and general notes.
* **Reminders:** Schedule follow-up dates. The system highlights upcoming follow-ups at the top of the client profile so you never miss a callback.
* **History Log:** View a complete timeline of your discussions and outcomes for each client.

---

## 2. Property Sharing System (One-Click Share)
Share property details with your clients quickly, professionally, and securely.

* **Share Instantly:** Click **"Share Property"** from any client profile to search and select a property listing.
* **Smart WhatsApp Share:** The system generates a beautiful, pre-written WhatsApp message with the property name, price, location, and a private link.
* **Client Portal (No Login Required):** Your clients can click the link to open a premium, mobile-friendly page showing:
  * Property images and thumbnail gallery.
  * Price, bedrooms, bathrooms, and carpet area.
  * Checklist of amenities (gym, parking, security, etc.).
* **Download PDF:** Clients can download a beautifully formatted PDF brochure of the property directly from the page.
* **Fully Secured:** Sensitive owner details, broker commissions, and internal CRM data are hidden from public view.
